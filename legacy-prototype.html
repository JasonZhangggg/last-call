    The Crooked LanternLast Call

    Three regulars, twenty cards and one revolver with a single round in it.

    1. Each round names a table card: Sun, Moon or Star. Everyone is dealt 5.
    2. On your turn, lay 1 to 3 cards face down and claim they all match. Wilds always match.
    3. The next player either plays on or calls Liar. The cards are flipped.
    4. Whoever was wrong pulls the trigger. Each revolver holds one round in six chambers, so the odds worsen with every pull.
    5. Watch their eyes. Some of them flicker when they lie. Some flicker anyway.
