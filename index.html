    The Crooked Lantern

    Last Call

    Four chairs, twenty cards and a revolver with a single round in it. Bring friends, or sit with the regulars.

    Lighting the lamps

    How to play

    1. Each round names a table card: King, Queen or Ace. Everyone is dealt 5 from a deck of 6 Kings, 6 Queens, 6 Aces and 2 Jokers.
    2. On your turn, lay 1 to 3 cards face down and claim they all match the table card. Jokers always match. Nobody checks unless someone calls.
    3. The next player either plays on or calls Liar. The cards are flipped.
    4. Whoever was wrong pulls the trigger. Each player's revolver hides one round in six chambers, so every pull you survive makes the next one worse.
    5. If everyone else has emptied their hand, you must call the last play.
    6. With Devil rules the deck holds one Devil, played alone. If a call turns it over, everyone but its player pulls the trigger.
    7. Watch their eyes. Some of them flicker when they're nervous. Every turn has a clock; when it runs out, the house plays for you.
