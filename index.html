    How to play

    1. Each round names a table card: Sun, Moon or Star. Everyone is dealt 5 from a deck of 6 Suns, 6 Moons, 6 Stars and 2 Wilds.
    2. On your turn, lay 1 to 3 cards face down and claim they all match the table card. Wilds always match. Nobody checks unless someone calls.
    3. The next player either plays on or calls Liar. The cards are flipped.
    4. Whoever was wrong pulls the trigger. Each player's revolver hides one round in six chambers, so every pull you survive makes the next one worse.
    5. If everyone else has emptied their hand, you must call the last play.
    6. Watch their eyes. Some of them flicker when they lie. Some flicker anyway.
